## 1. The one thing that matters most

**Nobody has shown that selective PAR2 antagonism reduces melanocore uptake.**

The historical pigmentation result (~80% reduction in melanin uptake, visible
depigmentation in Yucatan swine) came from **RWJ-50353, a broad serine-protease
inhibitor** — not a PAR2 antagonist. It blocks every trypsin-like protease in
the epidermis, and PAR2 may be only part of what it was doing. P2L-003, the best
modern orthosteric antagonist, has **never been tested on a pigmentation
endpoint**.

So the project rests on an unvalidated premise. **This gap is a larger
uncertainty than the difference between rank 3 and rank 12 on our candidate
list.** Everything computational below exists to enrich for molecules worth
testing; none of it can settle this.

The cheapest way to settle it is a reference panel — AZ8838, AZ3451, C391,
I-287, GB88 (biased-agonist counter-control), RWJ-50353 (historical positive
control), SLIGKV (agonist control) — run in a keratinocyte melanocore-uptake
assay plus a G12/13→RhoA counter-screen. **That is more valuable than any
molecule we designed.** It was not done: web access was blocked all session, so
no vendor, price or stock data could be obtained.

Also, do not overstate the ceiling: filopodial conduits, cytophagocytosis and
baseline phagocytosis are PAR2-independent, so **PAR2 blockade cannot abolish
pigment transfer**. The realistic aim is maximal suppression of the
PAR2-dependent melanocore route.

## 4. Defects found and fixed — read this before trusting older numbers

1. **Wrong AZ8838 tautomer** (found by an independent verification pass). The
   stored 8TZ was a non-aromatic 4H-imidazole. It had propagated into 394 of the
   3,000-design queue and 2,131 of the 15,000 library. Corrected; formula
   preserved in all cases.
2. **Receptor never protonated at pH 7.4.** Built with `obabel -h -p 7.4`, where
   `-h` overrides the pH model: all 39 lysines neutral, 17/29 Asp and 10/23 Glu
   as neutral acids. **No salt bridge was detectable anywhere in the campaign.**
   Fixed via `v2_fix_receptor_protonation.py` (39/39 Lys⁺, 0.000000 Å shift,
   original numbering transplanted because `-p` alone renumbers every residue).
3. **The anchor definition was wrong.** Ranking used ASP:228+HIS:135; the crystal
   shows **TYR:82+HIS:135**. The two populations are **95% disjoint** (Jaccard
   0.051, 7 molecules in common). ASP:228 had been justified by a code comment
   citing "largest modelled electrostatic terms" — **no FMO or mutagenesis data
   exists in this repository**. Retired.
4. **Which H-bonds appear is an artefact of preparation.** All three AZ8838
   imidazole protomers share one InChIKey (so no integrity check catches a swap)
   and each gives a different anchor pair; fixing receptor protonation removed
   the HIS:135 H-bond and added a HIS:227 π-cation. Only the *geometry* is
   invariant — three residues at 2.475/2.630/2.929 Å, next nearest 4.067 Å. Hence
   `v2_robust_anchors.py`, which scores heavy-atom polar distance and validates
   on the crystal at 3/3.
5. **Agonist-mimicry flag measured nothing** — two of its three residues sit
   8.7 Å and 10.6 Å from the ligand. Retired, not repaired.
6. **`GLY:157` in the 5NDZ profile was a dead key** (residue 157 is ALA), which
   silently capped that pocket's contact fraction at 0.8. Fixed.
7. **Stereochemistry destroyed by `TautomerEnumerator().Canonicalize()`** —
   2,534 designs, 3,042 centres, measured per step (`Cleanup`, `FragmentParent`
   and the protonation function destroyed none). The same call **changed the
   constitution of 1,199 designs** (mostly enol→keto) and **invented new
   unassigned stereocentres in 2,241**. 646 ranked finalists are affected:
   **207 were docked as the wrong stereoisomer**, 439 are ambiguous.
8. **The 24 "duplicate InChIKeys" contain zero true duplicates** — 6 genuine R/S
   epimeric pairs, 17 specified-vs-unspecified, 1 tautomer merge. One structure
   was docked twice (DN00314/DL03110) because dedup ran per batch.
9. **PubChem renamed `CanonicalSMILES` → `ConnectivitySMILES`**, silently
   discarding every similarity hit. First run reported 12 hits across 29 seeds;
   after the fix, 522.
10. **My autopilot v1 wasted ~8 h of billing.** On Linux `pgrep -c NAME` prints
    `0` *and* exits 1, so `n=$(pgrep -c gnina || echo 0)` produced `"0\n0"`, the
    numeric test errored, and the wait loop never exited.

**Not fixed (deliberate):** the dedup defect itself. Fixing it would invalidate
every committed `cand_id` mid-campaign. The correct fix for a future run is to
compute the dedup InChIKey **before** tautomer canonicalisation, and dedup once
over the whole library rather than per batch.

## 5. Statistical facts that should shape any future ranking

- **The scoring functions are not independent.** Within 5NDD: Vina ↔ ligand
  efficiency **−0.958**, Vina ↔ PIGNet2 **0.899**, CNNscore ↔ CNN_VS **0.988**.
  Interaction-fingerprint recovery correlates **≤0.19** with all of them — it is
  the *only* orthogonal evidence. Do not count six correlated scores as six votes.
- **PIGNet2 added little.** It correlates 0.899 with Vina and exists only for
  batch-1 rows. Not worth rerunning for symmetry.
- **44% of EX8 dual-anchor calls are sampling artefacts** — only 30 of 54
  survived re-docking at EX32.
- **EX32 is a resampling, not an upgrade**: 194 molecules improved on Vina, 245
  got worse, 96 unchanged, median change 0.0.
- **27% of batch-1 and 41% of batch-2 poses are sterically clashing** — GNINA's
  own positive affinities and PIGNet2 independently agree. A clashing pose can
  accumulate contacts and fake a high IFP, so clash-filter before selecting poses.

## 9. Traps that will bite you

- **Join only on `cand_id` or InChIKey.** `V2_LIGAND_TABLE.csv`'s `denovo_rank`
  is the batch-1 *queue* rank and matches the true library rank in only **52 of
  3,000** rows. **487** `dock_priority_rank` values occur in both batches and
  **all 487 name different molecules**.
- **Id prefixes**: `DN` batch 1, `DL` batch 2, `RX` real compounds. Never collide.
- **macOS `tar` injects AppleDouble `._*` files** that `*.sdf` globs will accept
  as ligand shards. Always `COPYFILE_DISABLE=1 tar czf`, and
  `find . -name '._*' -delete` after untarring remotely.
- **Shard granularity matters.** A shard only lands in `poses/`+`done/` when
  fully complete; at ~36 s/ligand a 100-ligand shard needs ~60 min. Use 25-ligand
  shards so results land incrementally.
- **GNINA picks output format from the file extension** — a temp file named
  `*.sdf.gz.partial` makes it write nothing while exiting 0.
- **`pkill -f v2_run_screen.sh` matches your own SSH command string** and will
  kill your session, orphaning `xargs`. Kill by PID.
- After merging lanes, boolean columns can arrive as object dtype, where `~`
  does integer inversion. Coerce with `.astype(bool)`.

## 10. Standing scientific guardrails

- Never call a molecule a PAR2 antagonist from docking, CNN or similarity alone.
- Keep 5NDD and 5NDZ evidence separate; never pool raw scores across pockets.
- CNNscore and CNNaffinity are ranking features, not calibrated probabilities.
- Missing values stay missing. Never write 0 for an absent measurement.
- The dangerous failure mode is **Gq-antagonist + G12/13-agonist** — it would
  still drive melanocore engulfment. Only the functional assay finds it.
- Label the output honestly: **COMPUTATIONALLY PRIORITISED PAR2 CANDIDATES**.
